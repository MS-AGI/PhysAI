# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PhysAI contributors and maintainers are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Help keep project spaces constructive and focused on the work. In particular:

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions, reviews, and other project interactions.
- Offer and receive feedback professionally. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and assume good faith while resolving them.
- Respect different levels of experience and make technical discussions accessible where practical.
- Follow the project’s contribution and security reporting guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable. This includes abusive or sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, unwanted attention, and publishing someone’s private information without permission. Repeatedly derailing discussions or targeting contributors after being asked to stop is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in PhysAI repositories and other project spaces, including issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, project events, and communication where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this code to **whataninfinity@gmail.com** with **"PhysAI conduct report"** in the subject. Include relevant links or context and describe what happened. Please do not post private details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical; information will be shared only with people who need it to address the report.

The project maintainer will determine an appropriate response based on the circumstances. Responses may include a request to stop, a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Retaliation against someone for raising a concern or participating in a review is not acceptable.

## Attribution

This policy draws on the principles and structure of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), adapted for this project.
